---
name: kickoff
description: Start-of-session routine for Kyūkyū-Bridge — load the working context and confirm it before any work. Use when the founder says /kickoff or starts a new session.
---

1. PROJECT_CONTEXT.md is loaded via CLAUDE.md. Also read HANDOFF.md and the newest "Session" section of
   PROJECT_CONTEXT.md. Read other files (ARCHITECTURE_EVALUATION.md, EXPERIMENT_LOG.md, COMPETITIONS.md) only
   when the task needs them; search FOUNDER_VOICE.md for the founder's own reasoning on the task's topic.
2. Confirm in 4 short bullets: where the project stands, what the last session decided, the open questions,
   and this session's task. Use the founder's answer style from CLAUDE.md (the why, an example, the trade-off).
3. Ask the founder for this session's two lines: the goal, and how we'll know it worked. Then propose plan mode
   for anything bigger than a small change.
4. Ask the founder in ONE line whether anything happened since the last session that belongs in `TRACKER.xlsx`
   (outreach, evidence, a number). Then keep the "Impact tracking" rule from CLAUDE.md for the whole session.
